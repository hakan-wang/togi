## What's in here

### `macos-screen-tracking/` — Michelle & Erik's hackathon experiment
A macOS desktop app (Swift). Its one notable idea is **screen-tracking**: a small floating icon
that lives on your screen and **screenshots it every few seconds** to guess whether you're on-task.

- This is a **super-beta, far-future** idea — *maybe* something Håkan's Togi explores one day.
- It is **not decided, not designed, and not on the current roadmap.**
- The actual product is the **web app** in `apps/web/` + the landing in `landing/`.
